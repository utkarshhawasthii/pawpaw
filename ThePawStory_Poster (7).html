The Paw Story 🐾
AI-Powered Dog Health Monitoring and Adoption Platform
B.Tech. CSE-VIII Sem. ★ Prashant Khetade ★ Manthan Krupal ★ Mohit Doye ★ Mrunal Raut ★ Nandini Dudhmogre ★ Minal Ghate
Introduction: "The Paw Story" is an AI-powered web-based platform designed to address challenges of both pet and stray dog welfare through integrated services. The system provides AI-Based Dog Health Checking, adoption support, and emergency reporting in one unified interface. Users can upload dog images to detect potential diseases, access verified adoption centers, report injured stray dogs, and purchase pet products through an online marketplace. The platform aims to improve accessibility, promote responsible pet care, and enhance community involvement — built using React.js, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and Machine Learning models.
Objectives
  • To develop an AI-based system for automating dog disease detection from images and symptoms.
  • To simplify the dog adoption process by connecting users with verified shelters and adoption centers.
  • To provide a centralized emergency reporting system for injured stray dogs with quick helpline access.
  • To offer an integrated online marketplace for dog food, toys, and accessories.
  • To encourage community participation through donation features and safety initiatives for street dogs.
Problem Statement
  • Animal welfare services are fragmented — no single platform integrates health, adoption, and emergency features.
  • Pet owners lack reliable tools for identifying dog diseases or locating adoption centers and veterinarians.
  • Stray dogs suffer from lack of timely medical attention due to absence of centralized reporting systems.
  • Existing platforms do not utilize AI for real-time health analysis or predictive disease insights.
  • Preventive safety measures for street dogs (e.g., reflective leashes) are rarely implemented at scale.
Methodology
  • Literature review and requirement gathering from existing animal welfare platforms and research papers.
  • Modular system architecture with independent modules: adoption, health detection, e-commerce, reporting, donation.
  • AI-Based Dog Health Checker uses ML models analyzing user-uploaded images and symptom inputs to predict diseases.
  • Secure authentication for personalized user interaction and safe data management throughout the platform.
  • User-centered design approach; testing conducted to ensure reliability, usability, and system performance.

Results & Conclusions
  • AI-Based Dog Health Checker provides high-accuracy disease predictions from images and symptoms, enabling early medical intervention.
  • Adoption module simplifies the process — transparent dog listings increased user trust and adoption engagement.
  • Emergency reporting system improves response times for injured stray dogs through direct helpline access.
  • Platform successfully unifies 6+ welfare services (health check, adoption, marketplace, donation, leash, reporting) in one system.
  • Community donation and reflective leash features received strong user interest, improving street dog safety.
  • Conclusion: "The Paw Story" is a scalable, high-impact solution ready for expansion with mobile app integration and NGO collaboration.
